Anti-patterns

crawler tooling drifts here by default
  • Terminal aesthetics — monospace everywhere, green-on-black, ASCII borders, scanlines
  • Neon accents, glow, glassmorphism, animated gradient backgrounds
  • Dark-mode-only, or a dark mode that is the light theme inverted
  • Decorative spider webs in the chrome — the logo carries the metaphor once
  • Colour used for emphasis rather than meaning; if everything is coloured, the one red row stops registering
